Middle Mouse Click Author: 1j01 Saturday, April 4, 2026 - 11:27 Art Type: Sound Effect Tags: mouse click button button click computer ui User Interface License(s): CC0 Collections: Preview: Middle mouse button press and release sounds. I held the mouse up to the microphone to record this - feels silly, but my theory was it would be further from my computer's fans - then applied noise reduction in Audacity, trimmed and ensured each sound starts/ends on zero with (unnoticable) fade in/out. These sounds will be used in Tracky Mouse, free accessibility software for using the computer hands-free.
