The Weight of Air – Anabasis I

Anabasis I is a slow, atmospheric work for strings. It has lots of textural, dissonance harmonies and elements that often feel quite suspenseful and spooky, but sometimes gives way to moments of calm and clarity – but never quite fully resolving. This work is part of the classical album ‘The Weight of Air’.
