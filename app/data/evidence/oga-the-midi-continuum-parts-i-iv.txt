The MIDI Continuum (Parts I-IV) Author: northivanastan Saturday, June 4, 2022 - 10:19 Art Type: Music Tags: midi downtempo orchestral retro License(s): CC0 Collections: Doom project music The MIDI Continuum (MIDI album) Favorites: 1 Preview: A four-part suite I made for my album "The MIDI Continuum." Goes through a lot of different styles, while remaining at a constant 120 BPM. I made these a long time ago, initially not publishing them because I wanted to add more than I ended up having, and then because it just didn't seem that useful for a video game, but here it is now.
