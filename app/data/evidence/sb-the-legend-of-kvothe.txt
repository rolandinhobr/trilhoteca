The Legend of Kvothe

An adventurous fantasy piece inspired by the protagonist from Patrick Rothfuss’ book series, the Kingkiller Chronicle (The Name of the Wind, The Wise Man’s Fear). Features plenty of ‘lute’ playing, starting out soft and sweet, but building up to a tavern showdown with the orchestra.
