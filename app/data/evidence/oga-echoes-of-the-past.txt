Echoes of the Past Author: isaiah658 Sunday, September 7, 2025 - 12:54 Art Type: Music Tags: somber dark weird creepy ominous strange mystery mysterious horror lost Ambient ambience ambiance seamless loop License(s): CC0 Collections: BlobGame Favorites: 8 Preview: I've recently found myself enjoying the music from the original Silent Hill games. What's interesting is that many of the sounds in those songs originate from a sample pack called Distorted Reality. This is the same sample pack used in the Spyro games and so many other pieces of media. In fact, I've attempted to mimic some of the sounds in the past with decent success. So I've decided to try it again except hitting that darker, thought provoking, mysterious tone. All sounds are made using ZynAddSubFX in LMMS. The song is a seamless loop. This is why there is a bit of bleedover at the start of the song and why it ends abruptly. Copyright/Attribution Notice: Credit to isaiah658 is not required, but is appreciated.
