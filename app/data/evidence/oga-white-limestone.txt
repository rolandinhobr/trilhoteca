Same chords as ( https://opengameart.org/content/osiris-monolith ) although different key. Pretty repetitious, so key changes periodically to break the monotony. You may or may not like that; at some point, I'd like to add real parts B, C, etc and cut back some of the key changes. A little backdrop: this isn't a sandy scene, but a sunset upon a desert painted black & white by an advanced civilization, and carved with their heavy machinery. Upon completion, every inch of the great pyramid of Giza was a reflective white limestone, sitting atop flat pavement made of carved black basalt stones, as far as the eye can see. This tune accompanies the creation of those pyramids, as I see it, as more evidence is beginning to show!
