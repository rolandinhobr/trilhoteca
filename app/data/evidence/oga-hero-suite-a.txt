Hero Suite A Author: Some Weirdo Sunday, June 15, 2025 - 13:04 Art Type: Music Tags: Character super heroes sad phrygian 95bpm slow cinematic License(s): CC0 Collections: Preview: Something for a project I'm working on. It's much more traditionally orchestral than I am used to but uses mostly the same instruments as 060. Except there's chelli now and I use a prophet synth (well, a vst meant to sound like a prophet synth called prophanity) and no FM synth. It's the most weirdly traditional thing I made yet. Edit: Now with lots more stuff! Old version available as "prevVersion". Changes below. Changed a lot of instruments, remade the melody in the "Danger" section to make the main melody more of a leitmotif. The whole second half feels different. Added an intro with much less going on. Better names in the zip file. This is intended to be broken up into short loops and used for different things within a game. At least things where the hero is supposed to be the centerpiece. All the chopped "pieces" to the song are in the zip file (including one that is not exported between the loop points).
