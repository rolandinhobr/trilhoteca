Awestruck Author: isaiah658 Wednesday, September 10, 2025 - 18:04 Art Type: Music Tags: wonder mystery mysterious bizarre strange weird creepy ominous horror Ambient ambience ambiance seamless loop License(s): CC0 Collections: CC0 BGM [Airos] [CC0] Music Favorites: 6 Preview: I've recently found myself enjoying the music from the original Silent Hill games. What's interesting is that many of the sounds in those songs originate from a sample pack called Distorted Reality. This is the same sample pack used in the Spyro games and so many other pieces of media. In fact, I've attempted to mimic some of the sounds in the past with decent success. So I've decided to try it again except hitting that darker, thought provoking, mysterious tone. All sounds are made using ZynAddSubFX in LMMS. This is the second song I've made in this style. There is a heavy emphasis on the instruments themselves rather than the notes being played. The song is a seamless loop. This is why there is a bit of bleedover at the start of the song and why it ends abruptly. Copyright/Attribution Notice: Credit to isaiah658 is not required, but is appreciated.
