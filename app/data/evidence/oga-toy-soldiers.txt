Toy Soldiers Author: Zane Little Music Monday, August 7, 2023 - 17:20 Art Type: Music Tags: VGM chiptune toys cute toybox silly happy fun soldiers License(s): CC0 Collections: A Test Art With (Open) Character Free Music Free music - CC0 music User-Supported Music Favorites: 16 Preview: Toy Soldiers march onward! Enjoy this free little tune that took me tons of time to make! If you like what I do and want some extra goodies, consider supporting me on Ko-Fi! It helps me focus more time on making free music like this. About the track: This track was the product of some long, obsessive days. I had a lot of fun making it though! I've had the idea for this song's concept for a little while now, and it's been a blast putting it into motion and seeing what happened! I focused a lot on sound design with this one. I recorded/augmented a lot of audio to get a lot of the SFX you hear in the song. For example, I had made the toy wind-up sound effects with my mouth and some car keys. I made a video demonstration of how I did it too! The toy's active 'motor' sound was also made with my mouth and a tin can. A similar amount of processing was needed to get it to sound the way it does, and I'm quite happy with how it ended up sounding! The goal was to make the song feel very tactile. A lot of this was achieved through both the texture of the percussion and instruments, as well as the mix. Many of the instruments were mixed to sound as if they were coming from a real object, and the track was mastered with a light room reverb. I kept it subtle though, because overdoing it sacrifices the details! Many of the instruments also have their own resonant frequencies, which I made to sound small, hollow, and metallic. I also pitched their resonance to the same key of the song as well, so even the percussive instruments do their own harmonic pulling too! In parts of the track that are meant to sound more 'real' (like the intro/outro), in addition to the room reverb/EQ, I also recorded some empty room tone to give it the effect of being recorded on a microphone. On top of the sound design, I also got very nitty gritty with percussion and rhythm design as well. I'm not sure if it's everyone's cup of tea, but personally, I like my music short and dense. (Probably the ADHD!) What the song lacks in length, it absolutely makes up for in girth! By that I mean detail, of course. After laying out the general outline of the song, I went through and filled in each section with their respective progressions and melodies, ramping up the layering as the track progresses. Then it was a process of scrubbing back over each section probably hundreds of times and breaking things into pieces and putting them back together. You could think of it like a mosaic. The fragments of stained glass are the original outline, and the fun little percussive doodles and melodic sparkles are the glittery glue that holds it all together. Anywho, I think that's probably enough rambling. I hope you guys enjoy the song! I had so much fun making it! I am pretty exhausted with it though. The sounds of the toys are going to haunt me until I move on to the next project. This song is royalty-free, so please have tons of fun with it! If you like what I do, please consider supporting me! I am always trying to improve the value for supporters as well, so if there's anything you'd like to have from me, please let me know and I can make it happen. The Deluxe version of this track (with loops, stems, sounds and more!) is available for free for my supporters! Have a great day!
