Scaling Up Author: Ruskerdax Friday, May 1, 2026 - 01:30 Art Type: Music Tags: upbeat Puzzle factory concentration flac License(s): CC0 Collections: Panda Disk System podcast tunes Favorites: 1 Preview: This upbeat track has been described variously as "concentration music," "Sim City-like," and "an alternative to the Satisfactory music." Happily composed using a Hohner Pianet Model N, Arturia Minifreak, and Roland TB-303, and makes use of the Mu-Tron Bi-Phase and a bunch of other instruments and sounds.
