Party and Gameover loop Author: gilzoide Friday, August 21, 2020 - 10:35 Art Type: Music Tags: loop loopable happy party sad GameOver game over License(s): CC0 Collections: Manic Minutes Public Domain Music I like Favorites: 5 Preview: A happy party theme with it's sister sad gameover theme, composed for the game Stack ( https://fog-icmc.itch.io/stack ). A simple crossfade between them when the game is over works well, as can be seen in Stack itself. The drum patterns were created using Hydrogen ( http://hydrogen-music.org/ ) and the melody and harmony sheet music were created using Musescore ( https://musescore.org/ ).
