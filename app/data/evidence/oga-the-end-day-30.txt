The End - Day 30 Author: Zane Little Music Wednesday, August 10, 2022 - 02:36 Art Type: Music Tags: digital fusion electronic electronic fusion electronic metal metal metal fusion License(s): CC0 Collections: A Test Dailiez Favorites: 2 Preview: Here it is. Day 30! I finished it at the crack of 12, and it took me all day. I wanted to make this last track something special! So I've sampled each of the last 29 Dailiez in this track, and made it as much of an adventure as possible! A 4-minute track is something I don't think I've ever accomplished in a day before, so I'm feeling pretty good about this! As for Dailiez, this is the end for now. I made a news post about it here if you're interested in learning more: https://zanelittle.newgrounds.com/news/post/1294141 All 30 tracks are free-to-use, meaning no copyrights or royalties or any of that stuff. Have fun with them! If you would like to support me, please check out the album on BandCamp! BandCamp: https://zanelittle.bandcamp.com/album/dailiez-vol-1 Thank you to everyone who stuck around for the ride and listened and supported me! There will be more to come.
